Brand guide

What Flow does, how it sounds, and which claims to leave out.

Positioning

Life, but easier.

Flow is a calm AI co-pilot for daily life. It brings priorities, habits, and spending into one place to help you decide what to do next.

Product proof points

  • Daily direction: Pick a priority and get a morning brief.
  • Routines: Track habits and recurring rituals.
  • Spending awareness: Log expenses and see spending recaps.

Writing voice

Calm, clear, practical.

  • Lead with a problem the reader recognizes.
  • Describe a specific feature or action in plain language.
  • Avoid guaranteed outcomes, invented testimonials, and promises about features not in Flow.
“Start with one thing that matters today. Flow helps you make room for it.”
